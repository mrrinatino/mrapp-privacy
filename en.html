1. Scope and developer

This policy describes data handling in the MrApp games listed below, distributed through RuStore, Google Play and other app stores. The developer is Rinat Iusupov, operating under the MrApp brand. Privacy contact: mrrinatino@gmail.com.

Features depend on the game, version and platform. The advertising section applies to versions with an advertising SDK; the Bluetooth section applies only to Russian Lotto. Applications with accounts, cloud services or health-data processing are outside this policy unless their package ID is listed below.

2. On-device game data

The games do not require registration with the developer. Progress, saved sessions, scores, records, achievements, preferences, virtual resources and internal counters are stored on your device. Some games allow a player name; it is used within the game and shared with room participants in Russian Lotto’s Bluetooth mode.

The games do not upload save contents to a developer-operated game server. The listed games do not provide developer-operated account cloud synchronization or a separate developer gameplay analytics service.

Local data remains until reset in the game, clearing app data or uninstalling. Some reset operations remove only part of your progress. Android backup and restore depend on device and app settings; restoring a system backup may bring saved data back.

3. Advertising and SDK data

Versions with ads use the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. To load, display and measure ads, the SDK contacts advertising servers. This may involve IP addresses, technical information about the device, operating system, app and connection, and ad loading, impression and interaction information. Where available and permitted by the app version and device settings, the device advertising identifier may be processed.

Processing supports ad delivery, selection and measurement and operation of the advertising service. Personalization depends on the app version, SDK settings, available information and user choices. Some games disable location tracking and the personalization consent flag or remove advertising ID permissions. This does not stop all technical communication with the advertising network.

The developer does not include local save contents, player names, email addresses or phone numbers in ad requests. Data is transmitted directly through the integrated SDK; absence of a game server does not mean that third parties process no data.

See the Yandex Mobile Ads documentation for SDK data types and protection in transit, and the Yandex Privacy Policy for the service’s processing and retention practices. We do not sell user data.

4. Access and permissions

Internet and network-state access support advertising and external resources. Advertising ID access depends on the build and system settings. Core gameplay is available offline; ads may not load without a connection.

The listed games do not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or access to your media library. Vibration is used by games with haptic feedback. Russian Lotto’s additional permissions are explained below.

5. Russian Lotto: nearby Bluetooth play

This section applies only to mrapp.rusloto. Nearby play exchanges player names, cards, drawn numbers, marks, readiness, connection state and results between room participants. A Bluetooth device address and a random token support connection and reconnection of an existing participant. This information is exchanged between room devices, not sent to a developer server.

The game uses system pairing and Bluetooth discovery, connection and visibility permissions. On Android 7–11, Android requires a location permission for device discovery; the game does not obtain coordinates or send them to the advertising SDK. Newer Android versions use Nearby devices permissions.

A service notification shows an active room and allows disconnection. Number narration uses an installed Android offline voice and does not record your speech. You start Bluetooth play yourself; you can revoke permissions in Android settings and disconnect by leaving the room.

6. Support communications

When you contact the developer, we receive your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them to reply, resolve the issue and handle data requests. Do not send passwords, payment details or other information unnecessary for your request.

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the request, resolve related follow-up issues and meet applicable legal obligations. On request, we delete support data unless there is a lawful reason to retain it. The email provider processes correspondence under its own rules.

7. Choices, deletion and data requests

To remove local saves completely, open Android Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data. Labels vary by device. This may permanently remove progress. To prevent system backups from restoring saved data, also review your device backup settings.

You can manage advertising identifiers and available personalization options through Android and device services. Restricting an identifier does not stop all SDK technical communications. You can revoke app permissions; related features may become unavailable.

For access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of previously given consent, email mrrinatino@gmail.com with the game name or package ID and your request. Requests are handled under applicable law. Requests about advertising-service data may also be directed through channels in Yandex’s policy. The developer cannot access your local saves; remove them on your device.

8. Children

The age rating and intended audience of each game are shown in its store listing. The games do not ask children for their names, phone numbers, email addresses or registration with the developer. Advertising versions involve SDK technical-data processing described above; we do not claim that no advertising communication occurs.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a support message, contact us to review and delete it where permitted by law.

9. This website and external links

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising SDKs, registration forms, our own analytics trackers or tracking cookies to this website. GitHub processes visitor technical information, including IP addresses for security, under its Privacy Statement.

When you choose to open an app store, scientific source, Yandex page or another external website, that service’s own rules apply.

10. Policy updates

We update this document when game features, data practices or disclosure requirements change. The revision date appears at the top. New features requiring a separate notice or consent will include that notice or request within the app. Policy questions: mrrinatino@gmail.com.
